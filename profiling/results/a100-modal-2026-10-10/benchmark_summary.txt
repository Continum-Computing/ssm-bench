Interpretation:
  These figures describe the reference implementations in this
  repo, not fused kernels. The SSM path is a Python for-loop over
  time steps, so at batch size 1 its latency is dominated by kernel
  launch overhead. Low utilization on both sides means neither
  implementation saturates the device.

  Nothing here supports or refutes an architectural mismatch. That
  question needs strong baselines (FlashAttention-2, official Mamba
  CUDA kernels, a fused Mamba block, CUDA graph capture) and energy
  telemetry. See docs/whitepaper.md.
